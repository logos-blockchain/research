retirement regime

Nothing pays a bonded miner to stop, and bonding does not switch the hardware off, so persistent is the honest default. Retiring is the optimistic reading both designs' headline numbers were originally quoted at — the difference is about threefold, and it is a behaviour, not a parameter.

Every simulated figure below comes from empowering_denovo_sim at the reference triple (0.5% of TGE, 25,000 nodes, 4 years), a field of whole Raspberry Pi 5 boards, and a whale at ten times the field it meets arriving at epoch 20 — its worst moment.
