The bootstrap, on this triple

Left: the endowment's schedule — linear to the expected end, the nominal-rate tail past it, and the dent a spike of the chosen size puts in it (the borrow-forward pulls later sub-pools forward; the schedule then re-spreads what remains, so the deadline does not move — a spike does not end the phase early). Right: the reward's admissible band — the demand-indexed price lives between one block's budget share and the anchor; where inside the band it runs depends on participation, which is the simulator's job, not a closed form's.

quantityvalue meaning

The post-phase runs on the previous epoch's diverted fees at the anchor — the cost of a transfer plus an inscription — so its capacity is pow_share × txs_per_epoch / 2: half the diverted transaction count, independent of the fee level. The throttle spreads exactly that many claims across the epoch.
